Ariadne

Privacy Policy

Effective 26 July 2026

Ariadne collects nothing. Your journal is written to your own Mac and stays there.

There is no account to create, no server to sign in to, and no analytics. The app makes no network requests at all — not one, not even to load its fonts.

What Ariadne stores

Everything you write — your entries, their dates, your threads and their colours, and your appearance settings — is saved in Ariadne's own private storage on your Mac.

That storage belongs to the app and to your user account on your computer. Nothing in it is transmitted anywhere.

What Ariadne sends

Nothing. Ariadne has no networking of any kind. Its fonts, styles and code are bundled inside the app, so it works identically with your Mac disconnected from the internet.

There is no telemetry, no crash reporting, no usage analytics, no advertising, and no third-party software development kits.

No account, no profile

Ariadne never asks for your name, your email address, or any other identifying detail, because it has nothing to do with them. There is no profile associated with your use of the app.

Exporting your journal

Ariadne can export your entire journal as a single file. That file is created by you, saved where you choose, and never sent anywhere by the app.

Once it leaves Ariadne, it is an ordinary document on your Mac. If you put it in a shared folder or a cloud drive, that service's privacy terms apply to it — not this policy.

Deleting your data

Delete individual entries inside Ariadne, or remove the app to remove its storage along with it. There is no server copy, so there is nothing to request the deletion of and no one to ask.

Export first if you want to keep your writing. Deleting the app deletes the journal.

Children

Ariadne is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

If syncing is added later

Ariadne does not sync between devices today, and this policy describes the app as it is now.

Optional syncing is planned. If it ships, it will require an account you choose to create, it will be end-to-end encrypted so that entries are unreadable on the server, and this policy will be updated to describe it before it becomes available. Ariadne will not begin transmitting anything under this version of the policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top will change with it, and the revised policy will be published here before the change takes effect in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy in Ariadne can go to cq.feedback@gmail.com.
